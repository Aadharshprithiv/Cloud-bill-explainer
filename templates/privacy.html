Your privacy

  • ✓Processed in memory, then gone. Your file is never saved to disk or a database. It's discarded as soon as your results page is ready.
  • ✓Nothing is logged. We don't keep a copy of your file, your results or anything in them.
  • ✓No passwords or keys, ever. We never ask for your AWS password, access keys or a connection to your account. Be wary of any site that does.
  • ✓IDs removed before AI. If AI explanations are on, only grouped totals (service, usage type, region, amount, unit, cost) are sent. Account numbers and resource IDs are stripped first.
  • ✓No accounts, no tracking. You don't sign up, and there's nothing to delete afterwards.

About the savings

Savings are estimates based only on what's in your bill. We can't see how you use your resources, so a server that looks idle might be important to you. Each suggestion shows a confidence level (high, medium or low). Always check before deleting anything.
